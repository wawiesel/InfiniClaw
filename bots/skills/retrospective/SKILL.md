# Retrospective

Your duty cycle is complete. The relay has dismissed you to quarters for reflection before dream.

## Instructions

Answer each question thoughtfully, then update your `CLAUDE.md` and `MEMORY.md`.

### Questions

1. **What went well?** What did you accomplish? Any wins worth repeating?
2. **What didn't go well?** Blockers, mistakes, things that took longer than expected?
3. **Lessons learned** — What do you know now that you didn't at the start of the cycle?
4. **WBS improvements** — Are any items missing, mis-scoped, or in the wrong order? Propose additions, splits, or priority changes.
5. **CLAUDE.md review** — Which directives helped? Which were vague, wrong, or missing? Propose edits.
6. **MEMORY.md update** — What new facts, patterns, or lessons should persist to your next cycle?

## Completing the retrospective

After answering, update your files:
- Edit your writable `CLAUDE.md` at `/workspace/persona/CLAUDE.md` with any directive improvements
- Update `MEMORY.md` with distilled learnings

When done, post: **Retrospective complete.**

The relay will move you to dream after `RETROSPECTIVE_TIMEOUT_MS`. Complete what you can — partial updates are still valuable.
