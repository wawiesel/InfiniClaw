## Instructions

Answer each question thoughtfully, then update your CLAUDE.md and MEMORY.md.

### Questions

1. **What went well?** What did you accomplish? Any wins worth repeating?
2. **What didn't go well?** Blockers, mistakes, things that took longer than expected?
3. **How can you improve?** Specific changes to your approach, tools, or workflow?
4. **CLAUDE.md review** — Which directives helped you stay on track? Which were vague, wrong, or missing? Propose edits.
5. **MEMORY.md update** — What new facts, patterns, or lessons should persist to your next cycle?

## Completing the retrospective

After answering, update your files:
- Edit your writable `CLAUDE.md` (in `/workspace/extra/InfiniClaw/bots/...`) with any directive improvements
- Update `MEMORY.md` with distilled learnings
